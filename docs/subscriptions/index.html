Will Bloons+ stay free?

Yes. Bloons+ is free forever. Pro is intended for additional tools; everything currently available stays in Free.

Can I subscribe today?

Not yet. The planned release date is 31 October 2026. Follow Discord for confirmed availability and the final feature list.

Is a lifetime plan available?

No. The planned billing options are monthly and annual.
