Pick the target

Automation separates map, difficulty and variation. A route must exist and meet its restrictions. A map can be unlocked in BTD6 while its route is unavailable in Bloons+.

Restricted modes

Primary Only, Military Only and Magic Only restrict tower types. Half Cash changes income. Alternate Bloons Rounds changes threats and timing. Do not assume a CHIMPS recording meets these other rules.

Dynamic maps

Moving platforms and temporary covers can prevent a recorded placement or upgrade. Follow failure evidence and modify a route only with a legal position and strategy in mind.

Victory evidence

Reaching the last recorded action is not a victory. The game must display its win and save the mode medal. Defeats and interrupted games remain distinct outcomes.
